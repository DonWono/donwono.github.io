The game

Run with a party, not a single life

Trinity Rush is an endless side-scrolling runner set in Goblin Forest. You start with Recruits and unlock fighters, rangers, mages, and rogues in the Tavern. Each class has its own mid-air skill and orb power. Distance, coins, and leaderboard scores are the goal — not a paywalled story.

Hazards include spikes, pits, crates, and goblins. Hits take out the current leader. The next party member takes over instantly. When the last hero falls, the run ends and coins are banked.

How to play

One-thumb controls

Press

Jump if you are on the ground. Coyote time and jump buffering keep the timing fair.

Press in air

Use the current class skill: slams, hops, shots, dashes, or drops.

Hold

Arcane classes can glide or hover. Release to drop back into the run.

The Tavern

Spend what you bank

  • Persistent coin wallet between runs
  • Unlock classes, party slots, and upgrades
  • Daily gift: watch an optional rewarded ad for 500 coins
  • Optional Game Center / Play Games leaderboards
  • Goblin Forest is the launch biome. Magma Tube and Deep Sea Trench are planned

Monetization

Ads and purchases, clearly

The game is free to download and fully playable without spending money. Advertising and purchases are optional extras.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Full-screen interstitial ads may appear after a party wipe on longer runs
  • Optional rewarded ads can triple a run’s coins or claim the daily gift
  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that skips wipe interstitials. Rewarded ads remain available if you choose them

Optional in-app purchases

  • Starter Pack — unlock Squire through Knight, plus coins
  • Pouch of Coins — 10,000 coins
  • Sack of Coins — 55,000 coins
  • Chest of Coins — 120,000 coins
  • Remove Ads — permanently skip wipe interstitials
  • No loot boxes. No randomized paid rewards. Prices are shown in the store and in-app.
