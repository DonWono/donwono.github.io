Trinity Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This policy describes how Donald Wono (“I”, “me”) collects and uses information in the Trinity Rush game on iOS and Android and on wono.dev. Contact: donald.wono.dev@gmail.com.

Official product page: https://wono.dev/trinity-rush/

Summary

Trinity Rush is a single-player fantasy auto-runner. You do not create an account with me. Game progress is stored on your device. The free game may show Google AdMob ads. Optional in-app purchases and optional Game Center / Play Games features are handled by Apple or Google.

Information stored on your device

The game stores coins, unlocked classes, upgrades, party setup, settings, purchase restore flags, and similar progress locally (for example with platform preferences). Uninstalling the app deletes this local data unless a platform backup or game-service save restores it.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Trinity Rush uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show:

  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads after some game-over screens on longer runs
  • Rewarded ads you choose to watch, such as tripling coins from a run or claiming a daily gift

Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising ID / IDFA or similar identifiers
  • IP address and general location derived from it
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Ad views, clicks, and approximate performance data

This information can be used to serve, personalize, and measure ads, detect fraud, and comply with law. I do not sell your personal information. I use AdMob so the free game can stay free.

See Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and AdMob’s current partner disclosures in any consent or privacy options shown in the app.

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS and Android settings. On Android you can also use Google’s Ads Settings. Where required, a consent form (Google UMP / IAB) may ask you to accept or reject non-essential advertising.

A one-time Remove Ads purchase skips wipe interstitials. Optional rewarded ads can still be shown if you tap them.

In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs, Starter Pack, Remove Ads) are processed by Apple or Google. I receive product identifiers and a fulfillment/restore signal so the game can grant coins, classes, or ad removal. I do not receive your full payment card number.

Game Center and Play Games

If you sign in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, those services may receive scores, display name, and similar social data according to Apple’s or Google’s terms. This is optional and used for leaderboards and related features.

This website

wono.dev is a static developer site. It does not require an account. The host may keep ordinary server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and delivery. The site does not run AdMob.

Children

Trinity Rush is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended as a children’s app under COPPA. Cartoon fantasy combat, advertising, and in-app purchases are present. Do not use the game if you are under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, email donald.wono.dev@gmail.com and I will delete it where I control it.

Your choices

  • Play without purchases. The runner, Tavern progression, and unlocks are available through gameplay.
  • Skip rewarded ads. They are never required to continue a run.
  • Buy Remove Ads to skip interstitial ads after wipes.
  • Stay signed out of Game Center / Play Games.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local progress.
  • Email donald.wono.dev@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests related to information I control.

Data retention

Local game data remains until you delete it or uninstall. Ad networks retain data under their own policies. I do not run a Trinity Rush user database.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date on this page. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
