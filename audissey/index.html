Audissey

Audissey turns web fiction into audio you can listen to anywhere — on your commute, while cooking, or with the screen locked.

Paste a chapter link from Royal Road or similar sites, and Audissey imports the story, cleans the text, and reads it aloud with natural text-to-speech. Build a personal library, organize stories on shelves, and pick up right where you left off.

In the app

See it in use

Listen your way

Playback that stays out of the way

  • Import stories from web URLs or pasted text
  • Play, pause, and skip chapters with lock-screen controls
  • Adjust playback speed to match your pace
  • Read-along mode highlights text as it’s spoken
  • Ambient listening screen for a calm, hands-free experience

Smart library

A library that keeps up

  • Auto-import checks for newly published chapters
  • Automatically split massive, single-page stories (like Project Gutenberg books) into manageable chapters
  • Search across stories and chapters
  • Organize novels into custom shelves
  • Track read progress chapter by chapter
  • Syncs seamlessly across devices

Built-in author support

When a chapter includes a Patreon or PayPal link, Audissey surfaces them in the reader so you can support the author — without those links being read aloud and interrupting your immersion.

Audissey Pro

Optional one-time purchase. Upgrade to Pro to remove ads, import unlimited chapters at once, and use Cache Ahead to pre-render Audissey Neural Voices audio so listening continues smoothly with the screen locked.

Voices

Intelligent, expressive voices

Choose Apple’s built-in voices or switch to Audissey Neural Voices, our on-device neural voice engine. All standard speech synthesis runs locally on your iPhone so your stories stay completely private.

Context-aware pronunciation

A custom heteronyms engine understands grammatical context, so words like “read” (present) and “read” (past) are spoken correctly.

Dynamic expression

The built-in expressive engine adds emphasis and pacing to action scenes and intense dialogue.

Custom dictionary

Define the exact phonetic pronunciation for unique character names and world-building terms.

Per-story narration

Assign specific voices to individual stories from their settings menu to match the novel.

Audissey Neural Voices currently supports English only. More languages are in progress.

Power users

Advanced API integrations

Connect your own third-party API keys directly on your device. Keys stay on the iPhone and are only sent to the provider you choose.

Smart speaker detection

Connect an LLM API key (OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic) so the engine can analyze chapters, identify characters, and map speakers to dialogue.

Ultra-premium audio

Connect your own ElevenLabs API key to stream ultra-realistic, multi-cast character voices into your listening queue.

Not another streaming subscription

Audissey is built for readers who love web fiction and want a simple, highly customizable way to hear their favorite stories. Import the chapters you already read. Listen offline. Keep the library yours.
